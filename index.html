The Lake: Road To Redemption

In this intense first-person psychological horror game, a father and son find themselves at the mercy of a psychopathic neighbor. Trust turns into a nightmare when a brutal betrayal rips the family apart. Left with nothing but grief, you must step into the dark to uncover the truth. Follow the clues, complete your objectives, and piece together the mystery to avenge your father. But stay alert you are not alone in the dark. If you hear footsteps, run. If they get close, hide. In this house, getting caught means death.

  • Genre: First-Person Survival Horror
  • Runtime: 15–30 Minutes
  • Core Theme: Betrayal, Abusive Power, Tragedy, and Drama
